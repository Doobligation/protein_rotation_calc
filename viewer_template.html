How to interpret this benchmark
  • This is a backbone-only implementation, not a reproduction of the paper’s all-atom calculations.
  • 1BA2 contains D67R; 2DRI is wild type and ribose-bound. Waters and ribose are excluded from this fitting model.
  • Bond lengths and angles stay fixed for torsion moves. Peptide ω and proline φ are fixed. Cartesian moves can distort local geometry.
  • The optimizer fits the known target; the animation interpolates fitted mode amplitudes. It is not a molecular-dynamics trajectory or a thermodynamic prediction.
  • Close contacts use a simple backbone-only 2 Å screening threshold and are not all-atom clash validation.
  • Timing is from this execution environment; three seeds sample fitting variability, not an extensive hardware performance study.
